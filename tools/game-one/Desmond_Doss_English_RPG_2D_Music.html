Chapter 1: Simple Present Tense

Move around the map. Find the wounded soldiers. Answer an English question to complete each rescue mission.

Move close to a red medical cross, then press E or RESCUE.

HOW TO PLAY

WASD / ← ↑ ↓ → Move
E / RESCUE: start a grammar challenge

Learning goals:
1. Simple Present Tense
2. Simple Past Tense
3. Present Continuous Tense

Scoring:
Correct answer: +10 points
Complete a rescue: +5 points
Wrong answer: −5 points
Use a hint: −3 points
Three wrong answers on one rescue = 1 missed soldier.
Three missed soldiers = GAME OVER.

Exploration:
Each rescue has a different question. The rescue area changes as you advance.

Historical note: the Medal of Honor citation describes approximately 75 casualties on the escarpment; Doss lowered the wounded to safety. This game uses 75 as its rescue target.
